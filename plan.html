A practical plan for Cheshire & Warrington.

Four priorities focused on the everyday things a mayor can help improve.

A plan built around how people actually live.

The new mayor should focus on the things that connect the region: transport, safer communities, a stronger economy and homes with the infrastructure people need.

This is the campaign’s starting point — practical priorities that can be developed with residents, businesses and communities across Cheshire & Warrington.

Better transport.

Make everyday journeys simpler, faster and more reliable — whether by bus, rail, road or active travel.

That means better local bus connections, tackling the bottlenecks that waste time every day and making sure major rail investment benefits the whole region.

See the local campaign

Safer communities.

Neighbourhood safety should be visible, practical and focused on the problems residents actually experience.

The mayor can use the convening power of the office to push for locally accountable policing, coordinate action on antisocial behaviour and support safer town centres.

A stronger economy.

Back the businesses already creating jobs here — and make it easier for new investment to choose Cheshire & Warrington.

That means listening directly to employers about skills, infrastructure and barriers to growth, while making sure the region has a confident voice with government and investors.

Hear from business

Homes & opportunity.

New homes should come with the roads, schools, GP capacity and local services that make growth work for existing communities too.

The campaign will push for development that makes better use of brownfield land, supports thriving towns and links new housing to real infrastructure and opportunity.
