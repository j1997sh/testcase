Ben’s plan for
Cheshire & Warrington.

A practical plan focused on the things a mayor can actually change.

Better transport Safer communities A stronger economy Homes & opportunity
Chapter one

Better transport

Make everyday journeys simpler, faster and more reliable — whether by bus, rail, road or active travel.

01

Better buses

More reliable routes, better connections and services shaped around how people actually travel.

02

Fix the bottlenecks

Target congestion and road pinch points that cost residents and businesses time every day.

03

Back Crewe as a gateway

Fight for rail investment that benefits the whole region and links people to jobs and opportunity.

Chapter two

Safer communities

Make neighbourhood safety visible, practical and focused on the problems residents actually experience.

01

Neighbourhood policing

Push for visible, locally accountable policing where communities need it most.

02

Tackle antisocial behaviour

Coordinate councils, police and local partners around repeat hotspots and persistent problems.

03

Safer town centres

Support thriving evening economies without accepting crime and disorder as inevitable.

Chapter three

A stronger economy

Make Cheshire & Warrington the easiest place in the North West to invest, grow and create good jobs.

01

Back local business

Put employers at the heart of the region’s growth, skills and infrastructure decisions.

02

Skills for local jobs

Bring colleges, employers and public investment together around the jobs the region needs.

03

Sell the region

Use the mayoralty to attract investment and champion Cheshire & Warrington nationally and internationally.

Chapter four

Homes & opportunity

Build the homes people need while making sure growth comes with the infrastructure and services to support it.

01

Brownfield first

Prioritise regeneration and previously developed land before losing valued green spaces.

02

Infrastructure with growth

New homes should come with roads, schools, GP capacity and public transport.

03

Revive town centres

Bring empty buildings back into use and create places people want to live, work and spend time.
