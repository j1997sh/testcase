A stronger economy.

Back the businesses already creating jobs here — and make it easier for new investment to choose Cheshire & Warrington.

Growth has to be built with local employers.

The region needs a mayor who listens directly to businesses about the barriers to growth, skills, infrastructure and investment — and then makes the case for Cheshire & Warrington.

This page sets out the campaign’s starting point. The detail should keep developing with residents, businesses and communities across Cheshire & Warrington.

What Ben would focus on.

Back local employers

Build a direct relationship with businesses of every size and make their priorities part of the mayoral agenda.

Skills that match jobs

Bring employers, colleges and training providers together so local people can access the opportunities being created.

Sell the region

Give Cheshire & Warrington a confident voice with government and investors, focused on practical opportunities for growth.
