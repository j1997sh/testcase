Safer communities.

Neighbourhood safety should be visible, practical and focused on the problems residents actually experience.

Safety starts with visible local leadership.

The mayor can bring councils, police and communities together around the places where problems persist — and keep pressure on until residents see a difference.

This page sets out the campaign’s starting point. The detail should keep developing with residents, businesses and communities across Cheshire & Warrington.

What Ben would focus on.

Neighbourhood policing

Push for visible, locally accountable policing where communities need it most.

Tackle antisocial behaviour

Coordinate councils, police and local partners around repeat hotspots and persistent problems.

Safer town centres

Support thriving town centres and evening economies without accepting crime and disorder as inevitable.
